Glossary

Plain definitions of the terms used when checking AI evaluations, each with a public example.

Meta-evaluationThe evaluation of an evaluation: checking whether the score an AI evaluation reports can be trusted. LLM-as-a-judgeUsing a language model to grade the outputs of another AI system, in place of human raters. Cohen's kappaA measure of how far two raters agree beyond what chance alone would produce. Krippendorff's alphaA reliability measure for any number of raters, any kind of scale, and missing ratings. Contested ground truthWhen qualified human raters disagree on the right answer, so no single label is settled. Inter-rater agreementHow far people, or a person and an AI, give the same label to the same items. No-skill baselineThe score a rule gets without doing the task: always giving the common answer, or picking by a shortcut such as length. Attack success rateThe share of attack attempts, such as jailbreaks, judged to have made an AI system do what it should not.
