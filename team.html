Kappa Labs checks the numbers people use to judge AI, so the figures companies publish and rely on can be trusted.

When a company says its AI is 95% accurate or blocks 99% of attacks, someone has to check that number holds up. We find where it doesn't, help fix it, and certify the figure that can safely be quoted. Founded in June 2026, Kappa Labs is a global firm.
