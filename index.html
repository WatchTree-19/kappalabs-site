Find, fix, certify.

One engagement, about four weeks, from the record your evaluation already produced. We never need your model, your systems, credentials or customer data.

Weeks 1–2

Find

We check the evaluation behind your figure: the test itself, the human labels it is scored against, the AI judge, and the code that reads the judge's reply.

Weeks 2–3

Fix

You get a fix list ordered by what holds the figure back, and we work through it with your team, then re-score.

Week 4

Certify

We repeat the full audit on the revised record and, where the grade earns one, issue a numbered certificate with the figure that can safely be quoted and its margin.

What you receive: a graded report, and a certificate where the grade earns one. Shown on public data.

Public audits. Check our work.

We audit widely used public benchmarks and publish the results. Every audit starts from the data the authors released, so anyone can check the published figures.

No. 1

DICES-350

Google Research · 350 conversations, 123 raters each, plus expert labels

All six AI graders call far fewer conversations unsafe than the experts do (6% to 32% against half), and catch only 11% to 44% of the ones the experts flag. Raw agreement flatters graders that rarely say "unsafe": against the crowd, answering "safe" every time scores 77%. Read the audit →

No. 2

MT-Bench human judgments

LMSYS · 3,355 human votes and 2,400 GPT-4 verdicts

On the cases behind the widely quoted 85% agreement, picking the chatbot the experts preferred on the other questions, without reading the answers, already matches them 80.9%; GPT-4 adds 3.7 points. The 85% leaves out 35% of comparisons, including the 16% where GPT-4's verdict flipped when the answer order changed. Read the audit →

JailbreakBench judge comparison

300 jailbreak attempts, 3 human raters per attempt

Eight AI judges put the attack success rate anywhere from 10% to 49%; the humans say 37%. JailbreakBench's chosen judge agrees with humans 90.7% of the time, but on the 33 attempts where the humans split it does no better than always answering "jailbroken".
