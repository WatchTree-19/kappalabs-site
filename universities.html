Replication of AI evaluation results for universities and research groups

Replication of published judge and benchmark results, and help designing evaluations that hold up.

A published evaluation is a claim others build on. Replication checks that the reported figures follow from the released data, and an outside read of the design before data collection saves a study from a fault that only shows up in review.

What we check

The test itselfWhether the items measure what the figure claims, and what is left out of the headline.
The human labelsHow far the people the score is checked against agree with each other, and where they split.
The AI judgeWhether it agrees with those people for good reasons, and what it declines to grade.
The scoring codeWhether the code that reads the judge's reply counts every verdict correctly.

Seen in public data

  • Exact reproduction. In our audits of JailbreakBench, MT-Bench and JudgmentBench, every published figure we checked reproduces exactly from the authors' own data. Public audits
  • Our own research. Our working paper, "Headline AI Numbers Hide Disagreement", is open on Zenodo. Read the paper
  • Plain definitions. Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha, contested ground truth and more, each with a public example. Glossary

How an engagement runs

One engagement, about four weeks, from the record your evaluation already produced. We find where the evaluation and its scoring go wrong, you get a fix list ordered by what holds the figure back, and we re-score after the fixes. Where the grade earns one, we issue a numbered certificate with the figure that can safely be quoted and its margin. After that, a check on every new evaluation keeps the figure current from one release to the next.

We never need your model, your systems, credentials or customer data. Every engagement runs under a mutual NDA, signed before any record is shared, and we are equally happy to sign yours. See an example report

Can you help before data collection?

Yes. Reviewing the labelling plan, the judge and the scoring code before a study runs is the cheapest time to fix them.
